# Jarvis prelaunch

Jarvis is the Lychee research assistant: same kernel in ChatGPT, Claude, and Cursor (MCP) and as **@lychee_data** on X.

Tag a market tweet, a headline, or a wallet. Jarvis looks up **Kalshi and Polymarket** on Lychee, lookalikes, holders when we have a book, and related news — then answers in the thread. In chat, call `jarvis_research` and land charts with `pull_market_bundle`.

This is **prelaunch**. Copy and matching will get sharper; the live path is already on.
