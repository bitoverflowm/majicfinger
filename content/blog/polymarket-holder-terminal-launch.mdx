# The Polymarket Holder Terminal Is Now Live

A Polymarket price tells you what the crowd thinks. The Holder Terminal shows you who the crowd is.

Pick any market and see its YES and NO holders, what they paid, and how skilled they are. Line up price moves with the news behind them, then follow any wallet into its P&L and new trades as they happen.

## What you can do with it

- **Know who's on the other side.** See the largest YES and NO holders, their entry prices, and how much of each side the top ten wallets own.
- **Separate skill from luck.** The Lychee Skill Score rates each wallet on the markets it has finished, so you can tell whether a side is backed by proven traders.
- **Spot the early movers.** New wallets, first-ever trades, and wallets holding both sides are flagged, so the wallets worth a closer look stand out.
- **Trade the why, not just the price.** Headlines from Google News and GDELT sit on the price chart, so every jump in the odds comes with the story behind it.
- **Follow the best wallets live.** Open any trader for their P&L, open bets, and finished markets, and get an alert when they make a new trade.
